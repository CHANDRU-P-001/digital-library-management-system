Frequently Asked Questions

How do I read a book online?

Open any book's details page and click "Read Online" — the PDF opens directly in your browser.

How do I save a book to my wishlist?

Click the heart icon 🤍 on any book card. It turns ❤️ once saved, and you can view all saved books under Wishlist in the navbar.

How do I change my password?

Go to Settings → Password & Security, and enter your current and new password.

How do I rate or review a book?

Scroll to the bottom of any book's details page — you'll find a star rating and comment box there.

I found a problem or have a suggestion. What do I do?

Send us a message using the form below. Our admin team reviews all messages.
